## Primary reference — the anchor for this project

**Link, S., Huang, X., Fernandez-Pello, C., Olson, S. & Ferkul, P. (2018).**
*The Effect of Gravity on Flame Spread over PMMA Cylinders.*
**Scientific Reports 8**, 120.
DOI: [10.1038/s41598-017-18398-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-18398-4)
Open access, **CC BY 4.0**. — *verified 2026-09 from article metadata.*

Source of: the BASS-II opposed-flow protocol; hardware and camera specifications; the
non-monotonic spread-rate result; the yellow→blue near-limit morphology description;
the µg/1 g limiting-oxygen-concentration comparison; the ~30 cm/s buoyant flow scale.
Nearly every L1 and L2 claim in FLARE-X traces here.

## Textbooks

- **Turns, S. R.** *An Introduction to Combustion.* — diffusion flame structure,
  conserved-scalar formulation, transport time scales.
- **Glassman, I., Yetter, R. A. & Glumac, N.** *Combustion.* — soot formation and
  diffusion-flame luminosity; the physical basis for reading a yellow→blue shift as a
  change in the soot contribution.
- **Law, C. K.** *Combustion Physics.* — Damköhler-number extinction theory and the
  S-curve.
- **Ross, H. D. (2001).** *Microgravity Combustion: Fire in Free Fall.* Academic Press.

## How to cite FLARE-X

> FLARE-X: an interpretable analysis layer for archived NASA microgravity combustion
> imagery (2026). NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project. Software, MIT licence.

**When citing results obtained with FLARE-X, cite the NASA PSI investigation and your
accession date as the data source.** FLARE-X is the instrument, not the data.
